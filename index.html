ABOUT EXO

EXO is a famous South Korean boy group formed by SM Entertainment. The group officially debuted in 2012 and became known for its amazing vocals, powerful dancing, memorable songs, and unique concepts. EXO has nine members: Xiumin, Suho, Lay, Baekhyun, Chen, Chanyeol, D.O., Kai, and Sehun. Some of their popular songs include "Growl," "Love Shot," "Monster," and "The Eve."

WHY PEOPLE LOVE EXO

  • Interesting Stories: EXO shares exciting stories through their music videos, often connecting songs with a special storyline about their powers and adventures. Their videos create a mysterious world that makes fans curious to discover more.
  • Unique Characters: Each EXO member has a unique personality, talent, and image that makes him special. Their original concept also gave each member a supernatural power, such as fire, water, healing, and teleportation.
  • Beautiful Animation: EXO's music videos feature stunning visual effects, creative scenes, and impressive transformations. These elements make their performances more exciting and visually appealing to fans.
  • Different Genres: EXO explores different music genres, including pop, R&B, hip-hop, and dance music. Their musical diversity allows them to create songs with different moods and styles that appeal to many listeners.
